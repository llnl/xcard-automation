# Contributing Guidelines

_Our contributing guidelines are open to change, just like everything
else in the repository. If you'd like to propose we do things
differently, simply follow the process to submit a merge request._

> **Note:** These are just _guidelines_, meant to encourage us in a
> healthy direction, but not intended to unnecessarily hamper
> development. Feel free to ignore any recommendation in the moment,
> understanding that doing so purchases a little bit of technical debt
> that may need to be paid back at some point in the future.

The details of our contributing guidelines can be found
[on our documentation site][site].
